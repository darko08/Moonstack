Moonstack

Build your tower to the Moon. Everyone sees how tall you are, and everyone wants to bring you down. One ward, eight rivals, one lunar cycle (about 15 minutes).

  1. Build floors. Each one costs more (floor price). Dust Nets above 60 m catch Moondust at night, ×3 on the Full Moon.
  2. Your foundation sets the safe height. To go higher, put up scaffolding: Leverage ×2, ×3 or ×5. You build faster, but hits hurt more.
  3. Margin Call means 2 hits to Liquidation. Add a Buttress, hang Stop-Loss nets, or take the scaffold floors down.
  4. Attack. Small titles (Moth, Toad, Hare) hit Wolf and Moon-Eater harder; a Moth raiding at night arrives unseen and plunders. Click a tower. Catapults drain the integrity of scaffolded towers. Sappers lower the foundation, which lowers the safe height.
  5. Tower Fall. When a tower is liquidated, debris lands on nearby plots. Send haulers first.
  6. Perigee Night. The Moonline sinks to 215 m. Towers above it land on the Moon: +50% score. Safe building alone stalls just below it.
